About these prices. OANDA's Brent and WTI quotes via TradingView, in US dollars per barrel. They track the nearest-month futures closely but are not official ICE or NYMEX prices or settlements. Oil trades from Sunday evening to Friday evening; outside those hours the last price is shown.

Brent is North Sea crude, traded on ICE in London, and prices most internationally traded oil. WTI is US crude, traded on NYMEX in New York and delivered at Cushing, Oklahoma. Brent usually trades a few dollars above WTI.
